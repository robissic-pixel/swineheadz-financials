Quant Methodology — how every number on this desk is calculated

MFI(14): Typical Price = (H+L+C)/3 · Raw Money Flow = TP × Volume · MFI = 100 − 100/(1 + Σpositive MF / Σ|negative MF|) over 14 periods. >80 overbought, <20 oversold.

Normalized performance: (Price / Session Open − 1) × 100, all assets rebased to a shared 0.00% baseline so a 2% BTC hour is directly comparable to a 2% SPY hour.

Ratio analytics: Ratio = Price A / Price B (scaled ×100 for readability on BTC pairs). Z-score = (ratio − 50-period mean) / 50-period σ. |z| > 2 = statistically stretched rotation.

CVD: Cumulative sum of signed volume — each bar adds volume when the pair closes up, subtracts when it closes down. Rising CVD under a flat ratio = quiet accumulation.

Correlation: Pearson r on aligned per-tick returns across all 7 benchmarks, recomputed every tick. r > 0.7 = moving as one trade · r < −0.5 = genuine hedge / decoupling.

Regime engine: Scores risk assets (SPY, QQQ, BTC, ETH) vs defensives (DXY, TNX, GLD-proxy flow) on 1H flow + MFI. Risk score ≥ +0.35 → RISK-ON · ≤ −0.35 with DXY/TNX bid → SAFE-HAVEN · extreme one-way score → FLIGHT TO CAPITAL / RISK-OFF CAPITULATION.

Flow estimate: Class flow $M/h = Σ (signed % move × dollar volume) per member asset — an impulse proxy, not exchange-reported flow. Use it for direction and ranking, not as audited flow data.
